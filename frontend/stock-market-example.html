How this example works
  1. 1Enter and open a pack. You get 24 players and pick 6: 1 goalkeeper, at least 1 defender, 1 midfielder and 1 forward, and 2 more outfield players of your choice.
  2. 2Every player starts at £4.00. Your £24 entry is split across your 6 players. 29 other entrants do the same.
  3. 3Press Next to play a gameweek. Each week you face one other entrant. When your player scores, assists, keeps a clean sheet or makes saves, the money comes from your opponent's players. Cards and goals conceded pay money to them.
  4. 4Survive the cuts. The bottom entrants are knocked out at set points. Their money is shared among everyone left, and each action is then worth more.

Where the money goes

Nothing is created or destroyed. When one of your players earns money, it is taken from your opponent's players who played that week, split evenly between them.

Bad events pay the other side. A card or a goal conceded takes money off that player and shares it among the opponent's players who played.

Didn't play? Value locked. A player with 0 minutes doesn't move at all that week: he doesn't earn, pay or receive anything.

Goals come first. If an opponent runs out of money, goals are paid first, then assists, saves and clean sheets.
