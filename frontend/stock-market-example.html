Stock Market

Try It Free. Real Results. Play Money.

Draft a 6-player squad and play through Gameweeks 1 to 6 with the real results. Every player is a shared stock: what one player gains, another loses.

How It Works
1
Draft your 6. 1 goalkeeper, plus at least 1 defender, 1 midfielder and 1 forward. 2 free picks either way.
2
Values move with real events. Goals, assists, saves and clean sheets push a player's value up. Cards and goals conceded knock it down.
3
Zero-sum, always. Say your forward scores a goal — he's awarded £3.00. That £3.00 is paid by your opponent's players who played this week, split evenly between them. A player who doesn't get on the pitch has his value locked for the week. The total pot never grows or shrinks.
