Four Tournaments

How Each Game Is Won

Predictions

Premier League Predictions

  • Predict the result and exact score of every match, every gameweek.
  • 10 points for the correct result (win/draw/loss).
  • 20 points total if you nail the exact score.
  • Points add up across the season — top of the leaderboard when the tournament ends takes the top prize.
Go to Predictions
Last Man Standing

Pick a Winner, Survive the Week

  • Each gameweek, pick one team you think will win.
  • A draw or a loss — you're eliminated. No result at all counts the same.
  • You can only use each team once across the whole tournament.
  • Last person still standing takes the pot — if everyone goes out in the same week, it splits evenly.
Go to Last Man Standing
Fantasy Manager

Build Your Squad

  • Pick a 15-player squad within a £100.0m budget — 2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD.
  • Choose one player as Captain — their points count double.
  • Every player's real-world points count toward your total, live.
  • Edit your squad freely up until each gameweek's deadline — highest total wins.
Go to Fantasy Manager
Stock Market

Draft & Trade on Real Form

  • Draft a 6-player squad — their value moves with real match performance.
  • Goals, assists and clean sheets push your squad's value up; cards and conceding pull it down.
  • Every gameweek you're matched head-to-head against another entrant.
  • Highest squad value when the tournament ends wins the top prize.
Go to Stock Market
