Historical Gameweek Sync

Pulls a REAL gameweek's actual player stats from the FPL feed into the local master database — not invented data. Once synced, rewind the master clock to GW1 and hit Advance Week to replay a genuine past season for testing every tournament type, Stock Market included, exactly like a real live season.

Player ID Watchlist

FPL occasionally reassigns a player's numeric ID to a different real player (usually once someone leaves the league). This checks automatically every time player data syncs and logs it here the moment it happens — so it can't silently affect a live tournament without you knowing.

Player Photo Verification

Checks each player's actual photo URL against FPL's CDN (a real request, not just whether a code is stored in the DB) and records the result. Players confirmed to have no working photo are automatically excluded from Bronze/Silver/Gold pack pools going forward — anyone not yet checked still shows up as normal, so this is safe to run at any time. Runs in batches; leave the tab open until it says Done.

Upload Player Photo

Fixes one player at a time. Pick a player below (list comes from the last Photo Verification run), click Search to open Google Images for them in a new tab, download whichever photo you want, then upload it here — it goes straight to storage and is live immediately, no redeploy needed.

Stock Market Zero-Sum Audit

Checks, on demand, whether every still-active entry's total value adds up to exactly what it should (entry fee × total entrants), AND replays every settled matchup/gameweek to confirm every individual player got exactly the right Won/Paid for their real actions, using today's live reward values. One click, everything checked.

Enter a tournament ID above to check its history.

Stock Market — Relegation Stages

Up to 8 checkpoints. At each one's trigger gameweek, the bottom N currently-active entries are relegated (out for the rest of the season) and their combined portfolio value is split evenly across every remaining player slot. The cost-per-action multiplier also updates from that gameweek onward. Leave Trigger GW blank on any row you don't want to use. A stage already applied can no longer be edited (shown greyed out).

Enter a tournament ID above and click Load.

Stock Market — Full Test Reset

One-click version of the manual SQL reset: wipes every Stock Market table (matchups, history, transactions, entries, stages, tournaments) across the board, creates one fresh tournament at Gameweek 1, and resets the shared master clock. This affects ALL Stock Market tournaments, not just one — same as the manual SQL you've been running.

Stock Market — Full Data Export

Downloads an Excel file with two sheets: every entrant's full per-gameweek player breakdown (same detail as the individual player history pages, for everyone at once), and a full log of every sell/buy transaction across the tournament — including pack fees, shortfalls, and how many other players' values absorbed each fee. Use the same tournament dropdown as the panel above.
