Stock Market

Draft. Trade Nothing. Watch It Move.

Draft a 6-player squad. Every player is a shared stock — their value rises and falls with real match events. It's pure zero-sum: what one player gains, another loses. No trading, no captain, just the market.

How It Works
1
Draft your 6. 1 goalkeeper, plus at least 1 defender, 1 midfielder and 1 forward. 2 free picks either way.
2
Values move with real events. Goals, assists and saves push a player's value up. Yellow cards knock it down. A red card wipes it to zero.
3
Zero-sum, always. The total pot never grows or shrinks — it only moves between players. Pick well, profit off the market.
